## 3. Five separate questions and estimands

Write rainfall p in the *duration-specific annual maximum rainfall* exceedance coordinate, p in [0.01, 0.50]. Let D be this admitted domain with mass M=0.49; the complement is rare 0.01 plus frequent 0.50, not assumed zero or equal to annual flood probability. For site s, duration d and fixed scenario, Q is nonnegative routed event peak in m³/s under the specified joint rainfall, loss, TP and preparation law. Define conditional descriptive CDF F_D(q)=Pr(Q<=q | p in D), strict survival 1-F_D(q), and attained/generalised inverse q_D(a)=inf{q>=0:F_D(q)>=1-a}. Conditional response-exceedance targets a=0.01, 0.02, 0.05 are proposed, **not annual flood AEPs**. The separate absolute known rainfall-coordinate contributions use original planned weights, and unresolved mass remains unresolved. A conditional diagnostic divides only by the fixed domain mass, never the surviving-success mass; do not change production v1 incomplete-tail results, which retain absolute weights, bounds and no accepted full-domain quantile.

## 5. Proposed confidence construction and acceptance

**28 November amendment (pending independent review):** the latent-ledger enclosure argument below supersedes the original observed-endpoint independence wording. The original merged draft and preflight receipt remain historical. See the separate [synthetic qualification](2026-11-28-issue148-synthetic-qualification.md); no physical execution is authorised.

Use separately fixed independent screening, reference and confirmation manifests. Generate independent uniform 64-bit root seeds with replacement for Python, then audit for duplicate roots and overlapping plan/sample identities; if a root collision is observed, stop with unresolved status rather than redraw or count a replay. Use the existing HMAC domain-separated plan/stratum/index/purpose identity. The within-run independent pseudorandom-draw model is a computational assumption, not a hash proof; explicitly review it for the existing sampler. The independent-root construction is for whole-cohort independence, not native equivalence. Never count plan extensions or replayed seed/ledger as independent. Native requires actual source-supported fresh-stream/independence and within-stratum law; timestamps or different printed draw values do not establish that. Document native and Python population differences even before inference. If strata differ, each needs its own weights/reference; cross-comparison is on the same scientifically admitted conditional estimand and equal attempted event budget. Do not pool unequal-weight observations into an IID KS or ordinary bootstrap.

**Reference design feasibility check (analytic, no simulation).** An initial conservative proposal used stratum-wise DKW with alpha_ref/(2*H*S*D). With 50 strata, two sites, one duration and 10,000 reference draws/stratum, alpha_h=0.00005 and epsilon=sqrt(log(2/alpha_h)/(2*n_h))≈0.023. Even a complete empirical CDF cannot reach 0.98 or 0.99 in that lower band, and paired half-widths exceed the proposed 0.01 probability margin. It cannot support the primary 1%/2% conditional quantile or CDF claims under the stated budget. Retain as a conservative diagnostic, not the proposed primary gate. Do not cure this by changing the margin.

**Revised proposed primary simultaneous reference region (finite frozen grid).** Per site/duration, use the 30 Python 1,000-event screening runs only to form a pooled weighted conditional diagnostic CDF (same law and original planned stratum weights; if incomplete stop). Freeze the sorted unique nonnegative thresholds: 0; its attained quantiles at nonexceedance fractions 0.01, 0.02, ..., 1.00; and, for each of the three primary pilot median quantiles m_j, max(0,m_j+k*Delta_j) for k in {-2,-1,-0.5,-0.25,0,0.25,0.5,1,2}. At most 128 thresholds/site/duration; include the exact three primary CDF thresholds. Freeze the same grid for both implementations before any reference/confirmation data. No adaptive insertion or post-reference tail extension. For 1.00 use maximum attained pilot Q; if any primary scale/threshold is unavailable stop.

For each implementation and site/duration, prespecify the complete latent conditional ledger from a common frozen law with independent draws across and within strata, including draws that may not be executed or observed. In each stratum pool only disjoint independent reference allocations; n_h is the complete planned positive reference count and v_h=w_h/M the known conditional stratum mass (sum v_h=1). The latent Q for every planned draw must be well-defined under the estimand. For exact Q or certified printed interval [l,u], I(u<=q) <= I(Q<=q) <= I(l<=q); use [0,+infinity] for all unknown draws, including unstarted, aborted, failed and unsupported draws, never exact zero. Apply Hoeffding to the independent **latent** indicators, not to potentially dependent observed endpoint indicators. On the simultaneous concentration event for latent empirical CDFs, the observed weighted lower/upper indicator sums enclose those empirical CDFs pointwise, even if observation/censoring or common caps are outcome-dependent. The radius is epsilon=sqrt(0.5*sum_h(v_h^2/n_h)*log(2/alpha_cell)); retain the conservative allocation alpha_cell=alpha_ref/K where K=2*sum_site,duration(number of frozen thresholds). Use complete planned denominators and known weights, clip bounds to [0,1]. Cross-output dependence is permitted by the union bound. If latent outcomes are undefined, their independent law is unqualified, or interval/weight enclosures cannot be established, no inference. Operational execution reliability is a distinct estimand requiring a separate frozen failure rule; do not substitute successful executions for the planned population or replace aborts. Reference estimands are population CDFs, not means of batch quantiles.

For 50 equal strata with 10,000 independent draws each, K<=512 and alpha_ref=0.01, the ideal illustrative half-width is about 0.0034, versus 0.023 for the rejected uniform DKW option. With unequal weights the effective count 1/sum(v_h^2/n_h) may be much smaller. Before activation calculate actual bound from admitted weights/counts and frozen grid size; if the proposed margin is impossible even with favourable CDF values, stop or seek a separately approved design, not a larger tolerance. This arithmetic is not a precision, power or throughput claim about M04.

At a grid point x, lower L(x)=lower-endpoint estimate minus epsilon and upper U(x)=upper-endpoint estimate plus epsilon, with endpoint definitions above (lower estimate from u; upper from l). Enforce monotonicity by taking the running maximum of lower bounds and reverse running minimum of upper bounds; reject inconsistent regions. For t=1-a, conservative lower quantile = max(0, greatest grid x with U(x)<t), upper = least grid x with L(x)>=t, otherwise unbounded. No interpolation or smooth-density assumption. Exact zero mass uses F_D(0) bounds. Ties/atoms near the target may widen the quantile region; report unresolved if it cannot bracket a finite margin. Between grid points the monotone region gives lower bound at previous grid point and upper at next, and beyond the maximum threshold use upper=1; this supplies a possibly loose whole-curve diagnostic. Primary population CDF comparison is limited to the fixed three thresholds and zero mass; do not equate this with whole-CDF equivalence. This can remain uninformative for rare quantiles, even if the ideal half-width looks feasible. Do not enlarge the grid or budget in response to reference data without a new independent protocol. For each confirmation cohort, use its actual conditional weighted empirical step estimate or conservative interval from the planned ledger (not a reference confidence interval); if incomplete, report indeterminate unless a definite failure can be established.

Total proposed family error 0.05: 0.01 reference region Python + 0.01 native; per implementation 0.0075 one-sided reliability lower + 0.0075 upper (four bounds total = 0.03). Reuse these simultaneous reference regions for all fixed transformations: CDF and quantile discrepancies, zero atom, and if later prespecified, envelopes. No extra pointwise 95% claims or confidence recycling. Unused native allowance stays unused for a Python-only claim unless re-frozen beforehand. Conditional on the fixed independently sampled reference region and pilot, classify each fresh production cohort as definite pass only when **every** primary output lies inside its margin for every admissible reference and observation value; definite fail only if no joint admissible values can meet all margins (a provably disjoint component suffices); otherwise indeterminate. Rectangular conservative checks can leave additional indeterminate outcomes. Report per-output one-run SD/CV/spread and error distribution across cohorts, with undefined CV at zero; SD/sqrt(R) is uncertainty in a mean, not one-run reliability.

Conditional on the independent pilot and on simultaneous reference coverage, definite-pass count <= latent true-pass count <= possible-pass count (including indeterminates), with the full prespecified number of cohorts retained, including unstarted/aborted cohorts as unknown. Under the fixed law and independent latent full-cohort draws, the latent pass count has a binomial law conditional on the pilot. Apply existing one-sided exact Clopper–Pearson lower to the definite count and upper to the possible count at their allocated alpha, by monotonicity of the endpoints; this does not assert that observed classifications are iid. The reference and latent-binomial coverage events are combined by union bound, not by conditioning the binomial coverage on observed classifications. If the latent success law or pointwise enclosure is unavailable, report unresolved. For 100 independent successes out of 100, lower at alpha=0.0075 is 0.0075^(1/100), approximately 0.952; this establishes feasibility only. Adequacy only if lower >= 0.95; inadequacy only if upper < 0.95; else unresolved. No dropping/replacing cohorts or ordinary CI peeking. Report native/Python difference region from the two independent population regions, including native print intervals; declare equivalent only if every predeclared discrepancy region is wholly within its practical margin, including fixed CDF thresholds and zero atom. A material or explained difference requires evidence and controlled attribution; non-equivalence does not establish a difference. No implication of exact replay or RORBwin compatibility from a conditional equivalent result.
